# Price Prediction Task

Our project is now formally defined as a deep learning classification system for short-term stock movement prediction. 
The primary objective is to predict the direction of a given stock on the next trading day, namely whether the stock will 
move up or down, based on its historical price information over a recent time window. At the overall project level, the 
main research direction is no longer centered on news or text modeling; instead, it is structured around a pure price-based 
time series classification framework. Accordingly, our core efforts will focus on price data collection, time-series 
feature construction, deep learning model comparison, unified evaluation design, and the development of a complete 
engineering pipeline. Within the team collaboration framework, our group is responsible for the price module, while 
the news group is responsible for the news module. The final system will integrate the outputs of both sides under 
a shared prediction target and a shared temporal alignment scheme to form the team's complete forecasting architecture.

In the data preparation stage, we will first use yfinance to collect historical daily stock data for the selected target 
stocks. The retrieved fields will include at least Open, High, Low, Close, Adj Close, and Volume, which serve as the 
foundation for all downstream modeling tasks. To ensure sufficient sample size and improve model robustness, we will 
collect data over an extended historical horizon and include multiple target stocks rather than relying on a single 
stock series. The purpose of this design is to transform raw daily market records into a sufficiently large pool of 
supervised learning samples instead of treating each stock as an isolated sequence. In addition, we will collect 
several market-related auxiliary price series, such as SPY, QQQ, ^VIX, and XLK, which capture broader market conditions, 
sector-level behavior, and market risk sentiment. Although the project is positioned as a price-only classification 
system, these external market variables remain part of the price side and can therefore be incorporated as auxiliary 
time-series features without changing the project's core definition.

In the label construction stage, the task will be formulated as a standard binary classification problem. For any stock 
sample observed at time t, the prediction target is the stock's direction on the next trading day. Specifically, the 
label is defined by comparing the closing price at t+1 with the closing price at t. If the stock price rises on the 
next trading day, the label is assigned as 1; if it falls or does not increase, the label is assigned as 0. This 
formulation is simple, interpretable, and well suited to serve as the unified target throughout the full modeling 
pipeline. To prevent data leakage, all input features must be constructed exclusively from information observable 
at or before the prediction time. No future information may be used at any stage of preprocessing, feature engineering, 
model training, or final fusion.

During the feature engineering stage, we will transform the raw OHLCV records into two distinct groups of structured 
inputs. The first group is sequence features, including daily returns, log returns, price change ratios relative to 
the previous close, log-transformed volume, and rolling statistics. These features form a multivariate time-series 
tensor organized over a fixed-length lookback window of 30 trading days, referred to as X_seq with shape 
(N, 30, num_seq_features). The second group is technical indicator features, including moving averages, exponential 
moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR, momentum, and volume ratios. These indicators represent a snapshot 
of the market's technical state at the prediction day t and are organized as a flat vector per sample, referred to as 
X_tech with shape (N, num_tech_features). This separation is deliberate: sequence features capture how prices evolve 
over time and are consumed by recurrent or convolutional sequence models, while technical indicators summarize the 
current market state and are consumed by a dedicated feedforward model. Feature engineering strictly avoids any 
normalization; normalization is deferred to the dataset building stage where it is performed only after time-based 
splitting, using exclusively training set statistics, to prevent data leakage. The auxiliary market series, such as 
SPY, QQQ, ^VIX, and XLK, are aligned over the same time window and incorporated as additional feature dimensions.

For model selection, we adopt four model architectures: LSTM, GRU, TCN, and a Technical Indicator MLP. LSTM is one 
of the most established deep learning models for sequential data and serves as the primary benchmark for financial 
time-series prediction due to its ability to capture temporal dependency structures. GRU is a lighter and more 
computationally efficient recurrent architecture that often achieves comparable or more stable performance than LSTM 
with fewer parameters. TCN represents a different modeling paradigm from recurrent networks; through causal 
convolutions and dilated convolutions, it models temporal dependencies efficiently while benefiting from faster 
training and more stable gradient propagation. These three sequence models all consume X_seq and each provides two 
output modes: a classification forward pass producing P(up) for standalone evaluation, and a feature extraction mode 
producing a learned hidden representation before the classifier head for downstream fusion. The fourth model, 
Technical Indicator MLP, is a feedforward network that processes the flat X_tech vector rather than a time-series 
window. Its primary role is not to serve as a standalone predictor but to encode the 27 raw technical indicator 
values into a learned feature vector that is dimensionally and semantically compatible with the sequence models' 
outputs. This is necessary because the cross-attention fusion layer expects all price-side inputs to be learned 
embeddings in a shared representation space rather than a mix of learned vectors and raw numerical features.

In the training and evaluation stage, all four models follow the same rigorous training protocol. The dataset is 
split strictly by time into train (70%), validation (15%), and test (15%) sets, ensuring models are always trained 
on earlier data and evaluated on later data. Normalization statistics (mean and standard deviation) are computed 
exclusively from the training set and then applied uniformly to validation and test sets, preventing any future 
information from leaking into the training process. Each model's training is driven by Bayesian hyperparameter 
optimization using Optuna with a TPE sampler. Across approximately 50 trials, Optuna searches over hidden dimensions, 
number of layers, dropout rates, learning rates, batch sizes, weight decay, and activation functions. Each trial 
trains with early stopping that monitors validation ROC-AUC, and an Optuna MedianPruner terminates unpromising 
trials early to save computation. After the search, the best hyperparameter combination is used to retrain a final 
model with a longer epoch budget, and the model checkpoint with the best validation performance is saved. The final 
model is then evaluated on the held-out test set using Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-score, and ROC-AUC. All 
four models are compared on the same test set under these unified metrics.

At the engineering level, the system is organized as a modular pipeline controlled by main_pipeline.py through 
boolean flags. The pipeline stages are: (1) fetch_data.py downloads raw OHLCV data via yfinance; (2) 
feature_engineering.py computes sequence features and technical indicators without any normalization; (3) 
dataset_builder.py constructs rolling-window supervised samples, performs time-based splitting, and applies 
normalization using only training set statistics; (4) each model file (lstm_model.py, gru_model.py, tcn_model.py, 
tech_indicator_mlp.py) contains its own complete training pipeline including Bayesian search, final retraining, 
test evaluation, and artifact saving; (5) metrics.py provides shared evaluation functions called internally by 
each model; (6) fusion_interface.py loads all four trained models, extracts feature vectors via each model's 
extract_features() method, and exports them alongside metadata for the downstream cross-attention fusion layer. 
Each stage saves its outputs to disk and subsequent stages read from disk, ensuring a clean separation of concerns 
and full reproducibility.

In summary, the workflow of the price prediction module forms a complete pipeline. We begin by collecting 
multi-stock historical price data and relevant market series through yfinance, constructing the raw price database. 
We then compute two groups of features—sequence features and technical indicators—without normalization, and build 
rolling-window supervised samples with time-based splitting and train-only normalization. Next, we train four models 
(LSTM, GRU, TCN, and Technical Indicator MLP) using Bayesian hyperparameter search with early stopping, evaluate 
them under unified metrics, and save the best checkpoint for each. Finally, fusion_interface.py collects feature 
vectors from all four trained models and exports them alongside prediction metadata, producing the complete 
price-side output that the downstream cross-attention fusion layer requires.

我们最终将整个项目正式定义为一个面向股票短期涨跌预测的深度学习分类系统，任务目标是在给定某只股票过去一段时间的价格信息后，预测下一交易日的方向，
也就是 up 或 down。整个系统的主线不再以新闻和文本建模为核心，而是以纯 price-based time series classification 为主体，因此我们的工作
重点将放在数据抓取、时序特征构造、深度学习模型比较、统一评估体系以及最终的工程化 pipeline 搭建上。在整体合作框架中，我们这边负责 price 
模块，news 组负责 news 模块，最后双方在统一的时间粒度和统一的预测目标上做结果融合，形成整个团队的最终预测系统。

在数据准备阶段，我们首先从 yfinance 抓取目标股票的历史日频数据。数据字段至少包括 Open、High、Low、Close、Adj Close 和 Volume，这些是所
有后续建模的基础。为了保证样本量和模型稳定性，我们会尽量覆盖较长的时间区间，并同时对多只目标股票进行抓取，从而将单股票时间序列扩展为一个统一的
多股票样本池。这样做的核心目的是把原始日级记录组织成足够规模的监督学习样本，而不是只停留在单条行情记录层面。与此同时，我们还会抓取与市场环境相
关的辅助价格序列，例如 SPY、QQQ、^VIX、XLK 这类可以反映大盘、科技板块和市场风险情绪的市场变量。虽然我们的核心任务是 price-only classification，
但这些外部价格序列本质上仍然属于价格侧信息，因此可以作为辅助时间序列特征纳入系统，而不改变项目"纯价格模型"的定位。

在标签构造阶段，我们会将任务统一定义为一个标准的二分类问题。对于任意股票在时刻 t 的输入样本，我们的预测目标是下一交易日的价格方向，
也就是根据 t+1 日的收盘价相对于 t 日收盘价的变化来定义标签。如果下一交易日价格上涨，则标签为 1；如果下跌或不涨，则标签为 0。这样的定义简单、
清晰、易于解释，也最适合作为整个 pipeline 的统一训练目标。为了避免数据泄漏，我们所有输入特征都只能使用预测时刻之前已经可观测到的信息，不能使用
任何未来信息，这一原则会贯穿数据处理、特征工程、模型训练和最终融合的所有阶段。

在 feature engineering 阶段，我们会从原始 OHLCV 数据中构造两组不同性质的特征。第一组是序列特征（X_seq），包括对前日收盘的价格变化率、
对数成交量、日收益率、对数收益率、日内波幅、开收差、成交量变化率、20日均线偏离度和波动率比等，以 30 天的滑动窗口组织成 shape 为 (N, 30, 
num_seq_features) 的三维时间序列张量，供 LSTM/GRU/TCN 等序列模型使用。第二组是技术指标特征（X_tech），包括 SMA/EMA、RSI、MACD、布林带、
ATR、动量和成交量比率等共 27 个指标，以 day t 当天的快照形式组织为 shape (N, 27) 的一维向量，供 Technical Indicator MLP 使用。这两组
特征的分离是有意设计的：序列特征捕获价格在时间维度上的动态变化模式，技术指标则总结了当天市场的技术分析状态。重要的是，feature engineering 阶段
绝不做任何归一化操作；归一化被推迟到 dataset_builder 阶段，在按时间切分完 train/val/test 之后，仅用 train set 的统计量来完成，从而
彻底杜绝数据泄漏。市场辅助序列如 SPY、QQQ、^VIX、XLK 也会在同样的时间窗口下对齐并以额外特征维度的形式并入输入。
在模型选择上，我们采用四个模型架构：LSTM、GRU、TCN 和 Technical Indicator MLP。LSTM 是最经典的深度学习时序模型之一，作为金融时序
预测的主基线；GRU 更轻量高效，在许多中等规模时序任务中能以更少参数获得与 LSTM 相近甚至更稳定的表现；TCN 通过因果卷积和膨胀卷积来建模时间依赖，
训练更快、梯度传播更稳定。这三个序列模型都消费 X_seq，并且每个模型都具备双模式输出：forward() 产生分类概率 P(up) 用于独立评估，extract_features() 
产生分类头之前的隐藏层特征向量用于下游 fusion。第四个模型 Technical Indicator MLP 是一个前馈全连接网络，处理的是一维的 X_tech 向量而非时间
序列窗口。它的核心价值不在于自身的预测能力，而在于把 27 个原始技术指标值编码成一个与序列模型输出维度对齐的、经过非线性变换的 learned embedding，
使得 cross-attention fusion 层能够在统一的表示空间中处理所有 price-side 输入。

在训练与评估阶段，四个模型遵循完全一致的严格训练协议。数据按时间顺序切分为 train (70%)、val (15%)、test (15%)，确保模型始终是用过去
预测未来。归一化统计量（均值和标准差）仅从 train set 计算，然后统一应用到 val 和 test，杜绝任何未来信息泄漏。每个模型的训练由 Optuna 
Bayesian 超参搜索驱动（TPE 采样器，约 50 个 trial），搜索范围覆盖 hidden_dim、num_layers、dropout、learning_rate、batch_size、
weight_decay 和激活函数。每个 trial 内部配备 early stopping 监控 val ROC-AUC，Optuna 的 MedianPruner 会提前终止表现不佳的 trial 
以节省算力。搜索完成后，用最佳超参组合重新训练 final model（更长的 epoch 上限），保存验证表现最好的 checkpoint。最终在 held-out test set 上
使用 Accuracy、Precision、Recall、F1-score 和 ROC-AUC 进行评估。四个模型在同一测试集上用统一指标进行公平比较。

在工程实现层面，系统以 main_pipeline.py 为唯一入口，通过 bool 开关控制各阶段的执行。流程为：(1) fetch_data.py 通过 yfinance 下载原始 
OHLCV 数据；(2) feature_engineering.py 计算序列特征和技术指标，不做任何归一化；(3) dataset_builder.py 构建滑动窗口监督学习样本，按时间
切分，并仅用 train set 统计量做归一化；(4) 每个模型文件（lstm_model.py, gru_model.py, tcn_model.py, tech_indicator_mlp.py）内部封装
了完整的训练流程，包括 Bayesian 搜索、final 重训练、test 评估和 artifact 保存；(5) metrics.py 提供共享的评估函数，由各模型内部调用；(6) 
fusion_interface.py 加载四个训练好的模型，通过各自的 extract_features() 方法提取特征向量，连同 metadata 一起导出给下游 cross-attention 
fusion 层。每个阶段的输出全部持久化到磁盘，后续阶段从磁盘重新读取，确保各模块解耦和实验可复现。

因此，price prediction 模块的工作流程可以概括为：首先从 yfinance 抓取多只股票及相关市场价格数据，构建原始价格数据库；然后计算序列特征和技术
指标两组特征（不做归一化），构建滑动窗口监督学习样本并按时间切分和 train-only 归一化；接着训练 LSTM、GRU、TCN 和 Technical Indicator MLP 
四个模型，每个模型都使用 Bayesian 超参搜索 + early stopping 找到最优配置，保存最佳 checkpoint 和评估报告；最后由 fusion_interface.py 
收集四个模型的特征向量并导出，连同预测 metadata 一起，作为完整的 price-side 输出交给下游 cross-attention fusion 层。
