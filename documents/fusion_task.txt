# Fusion Task

For collaboration with the news group and for final system fusion, we have decided to use a cross-attention mechanism 
as the final fusion layer, combined with a dual-task output design. The key idea is that the price module and the news 
module should not merely output hard labels or scalar probabilities and then combine them through a simple weighted 
scheme. Instead, both sides should output rich feature representations that can be deeply fused through an attention-based 
mechanism. On our side, the three price encoders—GRU, LSTM, and TCN—will each produce a learned feature vector for the 
same stock at the same prediction time. These three feature vectors are concatenated and projected through a linear layer 
into a shared price representation. This price representation is further combined with the output of a 
technical indicator MLP to form the complete price-side feature embedding. The news group will likewise provide a 
feature vector extracted from FinBERT for the same stock and prediction date. The fusion layer then 
applies cross-attention: the price-side embedding serves as the Query, while the news-side embedding serves as the Key 
and Value. This design allows the model to selectively attend to the news signals that are most relevant to the observed 
price context, rather than treating all news information with equal importance. After cross-attention, a residual connection 
and LayerNorm are applied to stabilize training. The fused representation is then passed into two parallel output heads. 
The first is a classification head, which predicts the probability of upward movement P(up) ∈ [0,1], trained with binary 
cross-entropy loss. The second is a regression head, which predicts the expected next-day return r̂, from which the 
predicted closing price can be recovered as Ĉ_{t+1} = C_t × (1 + r̂), trained with Huber loss. The joint training 
objective is L = α × L_cls + (1 − α) × L_reg, where α = 0.7 by default. The advantage of this dual-task design is that 
the classification head focuses on directional accuracy while the regression head provides a continuous magnitude signal, 
and the shared backbone benefits from both supervisory signals during training.

From the perspective of team coordination, this means that the most important alignment between our group and the news 
group is not the specific internal modeling details of each side, but rather the consistency of the external interface. 
Both groups must ensure that they predict the same stock, at the same time point, and for the same future target 
horizon. Both groups must also guarantee that only information available before the prediction time is used. Both 
groups must output feature vectors rather than only binary labels or scalar probabilities, so that the cross-attention 
fusion layer can operate on rich representations from both modalities. Once these interface conditions are 
satisfied, the two modules can be naturally combined. The full team system can therefore be understood as follows: 
the price-side deep learning module extracts trend and market-structure signals from historical price sequences; 
the news-side module extracts external event and sentiment signals from financial news via FinBERT; and the 
cross-attention fusion layer integrates both types of information by allowing the price context to selectively attend 
to relevant news features, producing a fused representation that feeds into a dual-task output consisting of a direction 
classifier and a return magnitude predictor.

In summary, the final workflow of the full team system forms a complete and coherent closed loop. We begin by collecting 
multi-stock historical price data and relevant market series through yfinance, thereby constructing the raw price 
database. We then define labels, build time-window-based supervised samples, and perform feature engineering to derive 
both raw price features and technical indicators. Next, we train and compare four price-based deep learning models—LSTM, GRU, TCN, and Technical Indicator MLP—under 
a unified temporal split, Bayesian hyperparameter search, and standardized evaluation metrics. After that, 
main_pipeline.py orchestrates the full experimental process and outputs each model's feature representations together 
with detailed evaluation results. Finally, under a shared label definition and consistent time alignment, the feature 
outputs of the three price encoders are concatenated, projected, and combined with the news module's FinBERT feature 
vector through a cross-attention fusion layer. The fused representation is then decoded by two parallel heads—a 
classification head predicting P(up) and a regression head predicting the next-day return—forming the team's final 
integrated stock movement prediction system.

## Cross-Attention Fusion and Dual-Task Output Detail

Cross-attention will serve as the final fusion layer of our team's stock prediction system. Its role is not to replace 
the price models or the news module, but to combine their outputs into one final prediction through a deep, 
attention-based mechanism that is more expressive than simple linear fusion. In practice, our three price-side deep 
learning models, namely GRU, LSTM, and TCN, will each produce a learned feature vector representing the encoded 
price dynamics for a given stock over the lookback window. These three feature vectors are concatenated and projected 
through a linear layer into a shared price representation. This is further enriched by combining it 
with the output of a technical indicator MLP. The news module will also output a feature vector
extracted from FinBERT for the same stock, the same prediction date, and the same target label alignment. The fusion 
layer then uses cross-attention, where the price-side embedding acts as the Query and the news-side embedding acts as 
the Key and Value. This mechanism enables the model to learn which aspects of the news information are most relevant 
given the current price context, effectively allowing the price signal to "query" the news signal for complementary 
information. After the cross-attention operation, a residual connection followed by LayerNorm is applied to produce 
the final fused representation. This fused representation is then passed into two parallel output heads. The 
classification head applies a sigmoid activation to predict P(up) ∈ [0,1], trained with binary cross-entropy loss. 
The regression head predicts the expected next-day return r̂, from which the predicted closing price can be recovered 
as Ĉ_{t+1} = C_t × (1 + r̂), trained with Huber loss to be robust against outlier returns. The joint loss function 
is L = α × L_cls + (1 − α) × L_reg, where α = 0.7 by default. This dual-task design is more powerful than a 
single-output approach because the classification and regression objectives provide complementary gradient signals: 
the classification head sharpens the decision boundary for directional prediction, while the regression head 
encourages the shared representation to capture return magnitude, which in turn improves the quality of the learned 
features. From an implementation perspective, the process is as follows: first, each submodule (LSTM, GRU, TCN, Technical Indicator MLP, and 
news encoder) is trained or fine-tuned independently to produce meaningful feature representations; second, the 
feature outputs are collected on aligned historical samples; third, the cross-attention fusion layer and dual-task 
heads are trained end-to-end on the fused features with the joint loss; and at inference time, the same workflow 
applies—each encoder produces its feature vector, the vectors are fused through cross-attention, and the two output 
heads produce a direction probability and a predicted return magnitude simultaneously.

和 news 模块不应该只是各自输出一个标量概率再做简单加权，而应该都输出丰富的特征表示，通过基于注意力的机制进行深度融合。我们这边的三个 price 编码器，
也就是 GRU、LSTM 和 TCN，都会在同一个时间点、同一个股票上输出各自的特征向量。这三个特征向量被拼接后，通过一个线性层投射到一个共享的 price 
表示空间中。该 price 表示进一步与 technical indicator MLP 的输出进行组合，形成完整的价格侧特征嵌入。news 组则在相同时间对齐规则下输出一个 
FinBERT 特征向量。随后融合层使用 cross-attention：价格侧嵌入作为 Query，news 侧嵌入作为 Key 和 Value。这一设计使模型能够选择性地
关注与当前价格上下文最相关的新闻信号，而不是对所有新闻信息一视同仁。经过 cross-attention 之后，再通过残差连接和 LayerNorm 进行稳定化处理，得到
最终的融合特征表示。该融合表示随后被送入两个并行的输出头。第一个是分类头，通过 sigmoid 激活预测上涨概率 P(up) ∈ [0,1]，使用 binary

使用 Huber loss 进行训练以增强对异常收益的鲁棒性。联合训练目标为 L = α × L_cls + (1 − α) × L_reg，其中 α 默认为 0.7。这种 dual-task 
设计的好处在于，分类头专注于方向判断的准确性，回归头提供连续的涨跌幅度信号，而共享的 backbone 同时从两种监督信号中受益，从而获得更高质量的特征学习。

从合作接口上讲，这意味着我们和 news 组之间最重要的协调内容不是彼此的内部算法细节，而是统一外部接口。双方必须保证预测目标一致，也就是都在预测同一个
股票、同一个时刻、同一个未来时间点的涨跌方向；双方必须保证时间窗口一致，也就是都只使用预测时刻之前可获得的信息；双方必须保证输出形式一致，也就是都
输出特征向量而不是单纯的 up/down 标签或标量概率，以便 cross-attention 融合层能够在丰富的多模态表示上进行操作。只要这三个接口定义一致，我们就
可以很自然地将两个模块连接起来。最终整个团队系统的结构可以理解为：price-side 深度学习模块负责从价格时间序列中抽取趋势信息，news-side 模块负责从 
news 中通过 FinBERT 抽取外部事件和市场情绪信息，而 cross-attention 融合层则作为深度且富有表达力的 meta-fusion layer，让价格上下文选择性地
关注相关新闻特征，产生融合表示后通过 dual-task 输出头同时输出方向分类结果和收益率预测结果。

因此，整个项目的最终工作流程可以概括为这样一个完整闭环：首先从 yfinance 抓取多只股票及相关市场价格数据，构建原始价格数据库；接着完成标签定义和时间
窗口样本构造，并通过 feature engineering 提取价格序列和技术指标特征；然后训练 LSTM、GRU、TCN 和 Technical Indicator MLP 四个价格侧模型，每个模型都使用 Bayesian 超参搜索 + early stopping，在统一的
时间切分和统一的 metrics 下进行严格比较；之后由 main_pipeline.py 统一组织实验流程并输出每个模型的特征表示和详细评估结果；最后与 news 组在统一
时间对齐和统一标签定义的前提下进行对接，将三个序列编码器的特征向量拼接投射后与 Tech MLP 特征组合，再与 news 模块的 FinBERT 特征向量一起送入 
cross-attention 融合层，
再通过两个并行输出头——分类头预测 P(up) 和回归头预测下一日收益率——形成整个团队的最终融合预测系统。

## Cross-Attention 融合与 Dual-Task 输出细节

Cross-attention 将作为我们团队股票预测系统的最终融合层。它的作用并不是替代价格模型或新闻模块，而是以一种更深层、基于注意力的方式将它们的输出整合
为最终预测结果，其表达能力远强于简单的线性融合。具体来说，我们这边的三个 price-side 深度学习模型，也就是 GRU、LSTM 和 TCN，都会分别输出一个学
习到的特征向量，用来表示某只股票在 lookback window 内编码后的价格动态。这三个特征向量被拼接后，通过一个线性层投射到共享的 price 表示空
间中，并进一步与 technical indicator MLP 的输出进行组合。与此同时，news 模块也会针对同一只股票、同一个预测日期以及同一个目标标签对齐规则，输出
一个 FinBERT 特征向量。随后融合层使用 cross-attention，其中价格侧嵌入作为 Query，news 侧嵌入作为 Key 和 Value。这一机制使模型能够
学习在给定当前价格上下文的条件下，新闻信息中哪些方面是最相关的，本质上是让价格信号去"查询"新闻信号以获取互补信息。经过 cross-attention 操作后，
通过残差连接和 LayerNorm 生成最终的融合特征表示。该融合表示随后被送入两个并行的输出头。分类头通过 sigmoid 激活预测 P(up) ∈ [0,1]，使用 
binary cross-entropy loss 进行训练。回归头预测下一交易日的预期收益率 r̂，由此可以通过 Ĉ_{t+1} = C_t × (1 + r̂) 还原出预测收盘价，使用 
Huber loss 进行训练以增强对异常收益的鲁棒性。联合损失函数为 L = α × L_cls + (1 − α) × L_reg，其中 α 默认为 0.7。这种 dual-task 设计
比单一输出的方式更为强大，因为分类目标和回归目标提供了互补的梯度信号：分类头锐化了方向预测的决策边界，而回归头则鼓励共享表示去捕获收益率幅度信息，
这反过来又提升了学习到的特征质量。从实现流程上看，这个过程如下：首先，各个子模块（LSTM、GRU、TCN、Technical Indicator MLP 以及 news 编码器）分别独立完成训练或微调，使其
能够输出有意义的特征表示；其次，在时间对齐后的历史样本上收集各个子模块的特征输出；然后，cross-attention 融合层和 dual-task 输出头在融合特征上
使用联合损失进行端到端训练；在推理阶段，也会使用同样的流程——每个编码器输出各自的特征向量，通过 cross-attention 进行融合，两个输出头同时产生方
向概率和预测收益率。
